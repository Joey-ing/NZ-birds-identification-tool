Bird name:Eurasian blackbird/
Manu pango

Habitat:City parks, farmlands, woodlands, and forest edges.

Size/weight:25cm/90g

Features:Adult males are entirely black apart from their yellow bill and eye-ring. Females and juveniles are mostly dark brown, slightly mottled on the belly.
